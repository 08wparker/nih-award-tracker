How the lag correction works

RePORTER publishes an award days to months after its notice date, so the most recent weeks are always undercounted and read as a collapse in funding. Each week's count is divided by the share of that week we expect to be visible by now, estimated by chain ladder from the reporting behaviour of recent cohorts. Weeks too incomplete to correct reliably are dropped rather than guessed at.

Fellowships are a different regime from research grants: an F32 is typically only half-published two months after its notice date, so its recent weeks stay masked far longer. The correction restores the level of a partially-reported week; it cannot tell you about a week nothing has been reported from.
